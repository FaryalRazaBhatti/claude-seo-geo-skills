# Voice

Save as `seo-workspace/voice.md`. Used by the planning and writing skills so drafts sound like you, not like a template.
The best input is real writing: paste 3 to 5 pieces you're happy with at the bottom.

## In a sentence

How would a regular reader describe the way you write?

## Tone

- **Formality:** (relaxed / balanced / formal)
- **Sentence length:** (short and punchy / mixed / long and detailed)
- **Person:** (I / we / neither)
- **Humour:** (none / dry / frequent)

## Things you do

Habits that make your writing yours. For example: open with a direct answer, use examples from your own work, end with an honest take.

-

## Things you never do

Words, phrases or habits you dislike.

-

## Your experience

What you can genuinely speak about first-hand. The skills only use experience listed here and never invent any.

-

## What must never appear in public content

Anything private: client names, employer data, personal figures.
